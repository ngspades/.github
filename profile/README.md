# Shoot and Destroy

**Ace of Spades, revived.** Build your cover block by block, dig under the enemy's base,
capture their intel, and earn a jetpack or a sentry gun along the way.

## The game

- **Singleplayer that is a real server.** Starting a match runs the dedicated server
  locally and joins it: offline and online are the same game, and friends can join you.
- **Bots that play like people.** They plan their way to the objective (digging,
  bridging, swimming out), take cover, hear digging, and split CTF into roles:
  defenders, infiltrators tunnelling with a bodyguard, snipers, rushers.
- **Classes and perks.** Commando, Rocketeer, Marksman and Miner, each with its own
  gear and streak perk: sentry guns, landmines, dynamite, revives, jetpacks.
- **Many modes.** Classic and next gen CTF, Babel, Arena, Zombies, Creative, and Lua
  game modes from plugins.
- **Up to 128 players a match**, bots included.
- **Gamepad first.** Every menu and every action works with a controller, for
  handhelds and consoles.
- **Still Ace of Spades.** The client keeps joining every public 0.75 / 0.76 server.
- **Everywhere.** Linux, macOS, Windows, iOS, Android, the browser and handhelds.

## Make it yours

| Repository | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| 🗺️ **[maps](https://github.com/Shoot-and-Destroy/maps)** | Every Ace of Spades map from [aos.party](https://aos.party) (891 of them), with the original pyspades `.txt` and a `.toml` ready for a Shoot and Destroy server. Copy a folder, add it to your rotation. |
| 🧩 **[plugins](https://github.com/Shoot-and-Destroy/plugins)** | Lua plugins for Shoot and Destroy servers: game modes, mechanics, admin and building tools, most of them pyspades / PySnip scripts ported to Lua. Includes the plugin API and examples to write your own. |
